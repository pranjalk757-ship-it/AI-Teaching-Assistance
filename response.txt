Based on the course material, here is what is meant by **mesh topology**:

### What is a Topology?
A **topology** is the arrangement of nodes in a computer network so that communication can be established among all of them.

### What is Mesh Topology?
In a **mesh topology**:
* Every pair of nodes has a dedicated line.
* Each individual node has a direct link to all other nodes in the network (for example, in a network with five nodes, a given node will connect directly to all other nodes).

### Advantages of Mesh Topology
* **Fault Tolerant:** A failure is not going to affect the network.
* **Reliable:** You can rely upon this network.

### Disadvantages of Mesh Topology
* The material notes that there are disadvantages to this topology, particularly when dealing with a large number of computers, such as a thousand or two thousand computers or more.
